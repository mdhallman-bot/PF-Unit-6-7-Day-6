Phase 1 · 0–15 minutes

1. Resume Detective: You're the hiring manager

Three students apply for the fictional grocery associate position below. Who deserves the interview, and why? Read the posting first.

FICTIONAL TRAINING POSTING · NOT A LIVE OPENING

Neighborhood Market — Part-Time Front-End Associate

Schedule: Afternoons and weekends; around 10–18 hours/week. Sample pay for this exercise: $18.50/hour (hypothetical, not a verified wage or offer).

Work: Welcome customers, organize bags and carts, keep checkout area tidy, restock light items, follow procedures, ask for help with difficult customer situations.

Required in this scenario: Clear communication, punctuality, ability to follow instructions, willingness to learn, and availability for some weekend shifts. Preferred: Customer-facing, volunteer, or team experience. Eligibility: Actual employers set age and task restrictions.

Applicant A — Jordan

Objective: Seeking a job.

Experience: I went to school and helped people. I am a very hard worker.

Skills: Nice, responsible, great at everything, expert cashier.

Availability: Not stated.

Applicant B — Taylor

Objective: Part-time grocery associate; available afternoons and Saturdays.

Experience: Volunteered at three school events; greeted visitors, arranged supplies, and helped clean up. Worked with classmates to finish tasks on time.

Skills: Communication, teamwork, following directions, organization.

Availability: Weekdays after 4 p.m.; Saturdays.

Applicant C — Morgan

Objective: Future CEO looking for leadership opportunities.

Experience: Managed a school fundraiser; oversaw a highly successful financial operation and directed a large team. No details provided.

Skills: Advanced leadership, executive decision-making.

Availability: Weekdays only before 3 p.m.

Need help interpreting the posting?

Relevant means connected to what the employer actually needs. Evidence is something specific in the resume that supports a claim.

Phase 2 · 15–25 minutes

2. Same person, different employer needs

Imagine the same student applying to both fictional jobs. A strong resume would still tell the truth, but it would target different experiences. You are not writing two resumes here. Compare the postings, then identify what each employer would want to see.

FICTIONAL PRACTICE POSTING

Job A · Neighborhood Market

Part-Time Grocery Front-End Associate

Schedule: Afternoons and weekends, about 10–18 hours/week.

Sample pay: $18.50/hour (hypothetical).

Work: Welcome customers, organize bags and carts, keep checkout tidy, restock light items, follow procedures, ask for help with difficult customers.

Employer needs: Clear communication, punctuality, following directions, willingness to learn, weekend availability.

Helpful experience: Greeting visitors, teamwork, volunteering, customer service.

FICTIONAL PRACTICE POSTING

Job B · City Recreation Program

Part-Time Activity Assistant

Schedule: After-school events and Saturday programs, about 8–15 hours/week.

Sample pay: $19/hour (hypothetical).

Work: Set up games, welcome participants, explain activity rules, organize materials, report safety concerns to supervisors, clean up after events.

Employer needs: Dependability, teamwork, clear instructions, following safety rules, willingness to learn.

Helpful experience: Mentoring students, clubs, sports, volunteering, events.

Notice: The applicant's real experiences do not change. The emphasis changes to match the job. Both postings are training examples, not active job openings; actual employers may have age or training requirements. Never invent experience or credentials.

Phase 3 · 25–55 minutes

3. MagicSchool: Create one resume for YOUR job

Choose one realistic job you could apply for now (not a future career requiring years of education or licensing). It can be one of the jobs above or something else that interests you. You will use this same position and final resume for your Brisk interview.

Open MagicSchool student login (class link). You are the author: AI helps you organize and improve a resume; it cannot verify your experiences for you.

Copy this starter prompt into MagicSchool. The AI should ask which job you want and work with you one question at a time.

Act as my high-school resume coach, not a ghostwriter. I am about 16–18 years old and need ONE truthful, one-page resume for an entry-level job I could reasonably apply for TODAY. FIRST ask me what specific job I want to apply for. If I choose a future licensed or professional career I am not qualified for yet, help me choose a realistic job I can apply for now instead. NEXT ask me ONE question at a time about the employer's needs and my real school, volunteering, clubs, hobbies, home responsibilities, or paid-work experiences. Help me identify transferable skills and tailor the resume to the job I chose. Never invent employers, job titles, dates, credentials, numbers, achievements, or leadership. If something is uncertain, ASK me. Do not request my real name, phone, email, school name, street address, or sensitive information; use [NAME] and [CONTACT] placeholders. Once you have enough facts, produce an editable, plain-text ONE-PAGE first draft. Then STOP and ask me to check what is accurate, useful, generic, exaggerated, or missing. Help me revise based on my feedback and explain why the changes better match my chosen job. Do not make a second resume. I need to be able to explain every claim in a real interview.
